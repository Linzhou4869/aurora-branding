## Change Record: Version 1.0 (2026-03-29)

### Summary

Initial release of comprehensive Ethical Sourcing Clause 4.2 establishing mandatory labor practice standards for all vendor contracts. This update replaces prior ethical sourcing provisions with enhanced, internationally-aligned requirements.

### Business Rationale

1. **Regulatory Alignment:** Ensures compliance with evolving supply chain due diligence laws (EU CSDDD, U.S. UFLPA, UK Modern Slavery Act)
2. **Risk Mitigation:** Reduces reputational and legal risk from labor violations in supply chain
3. **Stakeholder Expectations:** Meets investor, customer, and NGO expectations for responsible sourcing
4. **Industry Best Practice:** Aligns with leading frameworks (ILO, UN Guiding Principles, OECD)

### Key Provisions

| Section | Topic | Key Requirements |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| 4.2.2 | Forced Labor | Zero tolerance; recruitment fee prohibition; remediation requirements |
| 4.2.3 | Child Labor | Minimum age 15/18; age verification; education remediation |
| 4.2.4 | Working Hours | 48-hour week max; 12-hour OT max; 1 rest day per 7 |
| 4.2.4 | Compensation | Minimum wage; 1.5× OT premium; timely payment |
| 4.2.5 | Health & Safety | Safe workplace; PPE; training; incident reporting |
| 4.2.6 | Freedom of Association | Right to organize; non-interference |
| 4.2.7 | Non-Discrimination | Comprehensive protected categories; harassment prevention |
| 4.2.8 | Grievance Mechanisms | Multiple channels; anonymous option; 30-day resolution |
| 4.2.9 | Monitoring | Self-assessment; risk-based audits; third-party options |
| 4.2.10 | Enforcement | Severity classification; corrective action; termination rights |
| 4.2.11 | Subcontractors | Mandatory flow-down; vendor liability |
| 4.2.12 | Transparency | Annual disclosure; facility list; worker demographics |

### Comparison to Prior Standards

| Area | Prior Standard (v3.x) | New Standard (v4.2) | Impact |
|------|----------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Forced labor | Basic prohibition | Detailed with recruitment fee ban, document withholding prohibition | Higher compliance burden |
| Child labor | Removal from workplace | Education support + continued wages until legal age | Increased remediation cost |
| Working hours | "Comply with local law" | Specific 48/12-hour limits regardless of local law | Stricter than some jurisdictions |
| Wages | Minimum wage | Minimum + living wage aspirational goal | Potential cost increase |
| Grievance mechanism | Recommended | Mandatory with anonymous channel | System investment required |
| Audit frequency | Annual for all | Risk-based (1-3 years) | More efficient allocation |
| Subcontractors | Best effort flow-down | Mandatory with vendor liability | Expanded scope |
| Transparency | Voluntary | Annual disclosure required | Administrative burden |
| Non-compliance | Termination only | Tiered response with CAP | More nuanced enforcement |
