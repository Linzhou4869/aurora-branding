## 📋 AUDIT PREPARATION

### Pre-Audit (2 Weeks Before)

- [ ] **Notify Vendor** — Send audit notice (unless unannounced)
- [ ] **Request Documents** — Personnel records, time cards, payroll samples
- [ ] **Schedule Interviews** — Private worker interview room
- [ ] **Review History** — Prior audits, grievances, corrective actions
- [ ] **Prepare Checklist** — Based on Clause 4.2 sections

### Audit Day Checklist

- [ ] **Opening Meeting** — Management introduction; audit scope
- [ ] **Facility Tour** — All areas where work performed
- [ ] **Document Review** — Random sampling of records
- [ ] **Worker Interviews** — Minimum 10% of workforce or 20 workers
- [ ] **Closing Meeting** — Preliminary findings; next steps

### Post-Audit (Within 14 Days)

- [ ] **Audit Report** — Complete and send to vendor
- [ ] **Non-Compliance Classification** — Critical/Major/Minor
- [ ] **Corrective Action Plan** — Vendor submits within 30 days
- [ ] **CAP Review** — Approve or request revisions
- [ ] **Follow-Up Schedule** — Set verification date

## 📋 NON-COMPLIANCE RESPONSE

### Immediate Actions (Within 48 Hours)

| Severity | Actions |
|----------|---------|
| **Critical** | Suspend orders; escalate to leadership; legal review |
| **Major** | Written notice; 30-day CAP deadline; consider suspension |
| **Minor** | Written notice; 90-day CAP deadline; follow-up audit |

### Corrective Action Tracking

- [ ] **Root Cause Analysis** — Received from vendor (14 days)
- [ ] **CAP Submitted** — Received and reviewed (30 days)
- [ ] **Implementation** — Monitor progress (per CAP timeline)
- [ ] **Verification** — Audit or documentation review
- [ ] **Closure** — Formal close or escalate

### Escalation Triggers

🚨 **Immediate Escalation Required:**
- Forced labor or child labor confirmed
- Worker retaliation for complaints
- Audit obstruction or falsified documents
- Repeated same violation after CAP
- Life-threatening safety conditions

## 📋 QUICK FAQ

**Q: Do all vendors need Clause 4.2?**  
A: Yes, all vendor contracts must include Clause 4.2 regardless of size or risk level.

**Q: What if vendor refuses to sign?**  
A: Escalate to Legal and Procurement leadership. For critical suppliers, may require executive engagement.

**Q: How do I determine vendor risk level?**  
A: Use risk assessment tool in Implementation Guide Appendix C (geography + industry factors).

**Q: Can I customize Clause 4.2 for specific vendors?**  
A: No, Clause 4.2 is mandatory and non-negotiable. Schedule-specific requirements can be added but not as replacements.

**Q: What if local law conflicts with Clause 4.2?**  
A: Clause 4.2 standards apply regardless of local law. If conflict exists, contact Legal for guidance.

**Q: Who pays for audits?**  
A: Company pays for routine audits. Vendor pays for follow-up audits due to non-compliance.
