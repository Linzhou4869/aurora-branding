Vendor warrants all Deliverables will:
- Conform to Specifications and any samples provided
- Be free from defects in design, materials, and workmanship
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Be fit for intended purpose (where Company relies on Vendor expertise)

### 5.2 Quality Systems

[IF APPLICABLE:]
- Vendor maintains ISO 9001 or equivalent quality management system
- Vendor permits Company audits of quality systems
- Vendor provides quality certificates with each shipment

### 5.3 Non-Conforming Deliverables

For non-conforming Deliverables, Company may:
- Reject and return at Vendor's expense
- Require repair or replacement within [NUMBER] days

- Terminate Order or Agreement for repeated non-conformance

### 5.4 Product Liability

- Vendor maintains product liability insurance (see Section 10)
- Vendor responsible for recalls related to Vendor-caused defects
- Vendor cooperates with recall administration

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 6.1 Background IP

Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing Intellectual Property. No rights granted except as expressly stated.

### 6.2 Work Product

[SELECT ONE:]
- ☐ **Company Ownership:** All work product created under this Agreement is Company property. Vendor assigns all rights.
- ☐ **Vendor Ownership:** Vendor retains ownership. Company receives perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license for [SCOPE].
- ☐ **Joint Ownership:** Parties jointly own work product. Terms detailed in Schedule B.

### 6.3 Vendor Tools

Vendor retains ownership of tools, methodologies, and know-how used to create Deliverables, provided they don't incorporate Company Confidential Information.

Vendor indemnifies Company against third-party IP infringement claims arising from Deliverables, including:
- Defense costs and attorneys' fees
- Damages and settlements
- Costs to modify Deliverables to be non-infringing
- Refund if Deliverables cannot be made non-infringing

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

### 7.1 Obligations

Receiving Party shall:
- Use Confidential Information solely for Agreement purposes
- Protect with same degree of care as own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
- Limit access to employees/contractors with need to know
- Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent

### 7.2 Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known (not through breach)
- Was rightfully known prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is rightfully received from third party without restriction

### 7.3 Compelled Disclosure

If legally compelled to disclose, Receiving Party shall:
- Provide prompt notice (if legally permitted)
- Disclose only minimum required
- Cooperate with disclosing Party's efforts to protect confidentiality

### 7.4 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive for [NUMBER] years from Effective Date or [NUMBER] years from termination, whichever is longer. Trade secrets protected indefinitely.

### 7.5 Return/Destruction

Upon termination or request, Receiving Party shall return or destroy Confidential Information and certify compliance.

## 9. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

### 9.1 Indemnification by Vendor

Vendor indemnifies Company against claims arising from:
- Vendor negligence or willful misconduct
- Breach of warranties or representations
- IP infringement (see Section 6.4)
- Violation of laws (including labor and ethical sourcing violations)
- Bodily injury or property damage caused by Vendor

### 9.2 Indemnification by Company

Company indemnifies Vendor against claims arising from:
- Company negligence or willful misconduct
- Breach of this Agreement by Company
- Company's use of Deliverables outside Specifications

### 9.3 Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR EXCLUDED CLAIMS, NEITHER PARTY LIABLE FOR:
- INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES
- LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, DATA, OR BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES

**Liability Cap:** Except for Excluded Claims, total liability limited to [AMOUNT or "12 months of payments under this Agreement"].

### 9.4 Excluded Claims

Limitations do not apply to:
- Indemnification obligations
- Breach of confidentiality
- IP infringement
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Death or personal injury
- Fraud or criminal acts

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 11.1 Term

This Agreement begins on Effective Date and continues for [NUMBER] years, unless terminated earlier.

### 11.2 Renewal

[SELECT ONE:]
- ☐ Automatically renews for successive [NUMBER]-year periods unless either Party gives [NUMBER] days notice
- ☐ May be renewed by mutual written agreement
- ☐ No renewal; Agreement expires on Expiration Date

### 11.3 Termination for Convenience

Company may terminate for convenience with [NUMBER] days written notice. Vendor compensated for work performed through termination date.

### 11.4 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate for material breach if:
- Breach not cured within [NUMBER] days of written notice
- Breach is incurable or repeated after cure

### 11.5 Immediate Termination

Company may terminate immediately if Vendor:
- Becomes insolvent or files bankruptcy
- Ceases business operations
- Violates Ethical Sourcing Clause 4.2 (critical violations)
- Engages in fraud or criminal conduct related to Agreement

### 11.6 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:
- Outstanding Orders may be completed or cancelled at Company option
- Payment due for accepted Deliverables
- Return/destruction of Confidential Information
- Survival of Sections 6, 7, 8.3, 9, 12, and 13

## 12. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 12.1 Governing Law

This Agreement governed by laws of [JURISDICTION], without regard to conflict of laws principles.

### 12.2 Dispute Resolution

[SELECT ONE:]
- ☐ **Litigation:** Courts of [JURISDICTION] have exclusive jurisdiction
- ☐ **Arbitration:** Binding arbitration under [RULES] in [LOCATION]
- ☐ **Mediation then Arbitration:** Good faith mediation, then arbitration if unresolved

### 12.3 Notices

All notices in writing to addresses above (or updated addresses) via:
- Personal delivery (effective upon receipt)
- Registered mail (effective 3 days after mailing)
- Reputable courier (effective upon delivery confirmation)
- Email with confirmation (effective upon receipt confirmation)

### 12.4 Assignment

Vendor may not assign without Company prior written consent. Company may assign to Affiliate or successor. Any attempted assignment in violation is void.

### 12.5 Subcontracting

[SELECT ONE:]
- ☐ Vendor may not subcontract without prior written consent
- ☐ Vendor may subcontract with notice; remains fully liable
- ☐ Vendor may subcontract critical functions only with approval

### 12.6 Force Majeure

Neither Party liable for delays due to Force Majeure if:
- Notice given within [NUMBER] days
- Reasonable efforts to mitigate
- Performance resumes when condition ends
- Condition lasting more than [NUMBER] days allows termination

### 12.7 Severability

If any provision held unenforceable, it shall be modified to minimum extent necessary to be enforceable. Remaining provisions unaffected.

### 12.8 Waiver

Failure to enforce any right is not a waiver. Waivers must be in writing and signed.

### 12.9 Entire Agreement

This Agreement (including Schedules) is the entire agreement and supersedes all prior agreements regarding the subject matter.

### 12.10 Amendment

Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties.

### 12.11 Counterparts

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each deemed an original. Electronic signatures are valid.

### 12.12 Publicity

Neither Party may issue press releases or public statements about this relationship without prior written consent, except as required by law.

### 12.13 Compliance with Laws

Both Parties shall comply with all applicable laws, including:
- Anti-corruption (FCPA, UK Bribery Act, local anti-bribery laws)
- Trade sanctions and export controls
- Anti-trust/competition laws
- Data protection and privacy laws
- **Labor and ethical sourcing laws (see Attachment C)**

### 12.14 Audit Rights

Company may audit Vendor's records related to:
- Pricing and charges (with [NUMBER] days notice, during business hours)
- Compliance with this Agreement (including Ethical Sourcing Clause 4.2)
- Security and data protection (if applicable)

### 12.15 Relationship of Parties

Parties are independent contractors. No agency, partnership, or joint venture created.

### 12.16 Third-Party Beneficiaries

No third-party beneficiaries except as expressly stated.

### 12.17 Survival

Sections 1, 6, 7, 8.3, 9, 10, 11.6, 12, and 13 survive termination.
