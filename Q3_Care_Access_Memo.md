---

## Purpose

This memo summarizes key findings from our Q3 2025 patient feedback and clinical outcomes analysis, and outlines three priority actions we will implement in Q4 to reduce barriers to care and improve patient outcomes.

---

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 24 patient feedback responses and 28 clinical records reveals four major barrier categories affecting our patients:

| Barrier | Frequency | Impact on Adherence |
|---------|-----------|---------------------|
| Technology Challenges | 33% | 86% non-adherent |
| Language/Interpreter | 25% | 100% non-adherent |
| Scheduling Conflicts | 21% | 75% non-adherent |
| Transportation | 21% | 50% non-adherent |

**Critical Finding:** Patients with language or technology barriers had 100% non-adherence to follow-up appointments. Patients with no barriers achieved 100% adherence.

**Virtual Visit Outcomes:**
- Diabetes patients on virtual visits: 0% adherence, avg A1C 8.09%
- Hypertension patients on virtual visits: 50% missed follow-ups
- Overall virtual visit miss rate: 64% vs 50% for in-person

---

## Priority Actions for Q4 2026

Based on impact potential and feasibility, we are implementing the following three improvements:

---

### 🎯 Priority 1: Expand Interpreter Availability & Reliability

**Why This Matters:**
- Language barriers showed the strongest correlation with care discontinuity
- Patients waited up to 2 weeks for interpreter availability
- Family members (including children) were used as interpreters, compromising privacy and care quality

**Actions:**

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Audit current interpreter coverage gaps by language | Care Coordination Lead | Week 1-2 |
| Contract with backup interpreter service for overflow | Operations Manager | Week 3-4 |
| Implement pre-visit language confirmation call 48hrs before appointment | Front Desk Supervisor | Week 2 |
| Test and upgrade phone interpreter audio quality | IT + Clinical Ops | Week 3-5 |

**What Frontline Staff Needs to Know:**
- **Scheduling:** Always confirm interpreter needs at booking AND 48 hours before visit
- **If interpreter is unavailable:** Escalate to supervisor immediately—do not reschedule without approval
- **Documentation:** Note interpreter issues in patient record with specific language needed

**Success Metric:** Reduce interpreter-related missed appointments by 50% by end of Q4

---

### 🎯 Priority 2: Technology Support for Virtual Visits

**Why This Matters:**
- Technology barriers affected 1 in 3 patients (highest frequency)
- Rural patients and elderly patients disproportionately impacted
- Internet connectivity issues caused patients to miss appointments entirely

**Actions:**

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Create "Tech Check" pre-visit call option for first-time virtual patients | IT + Nursing | Week 2-3 |
| Enable phone-only visit option when video fails | Clinical Director | Week 1 |
| Develop simplified check-in process (no app download required) | IT | Week 4-6 |
| Create patient tech support hotline (staffed during clinic hours) | Front Desk | Week 3 |

**What Frontline Staff Needs to Know:**
- **At scheduling:** Ask "Do you have a device and internet connection for video visits?" Document answer
- **If patient reports tech issues:** Offer phone visit option immediately—don't wait for video to fail
- **Pre-visit:** For patients 65+ or who report tech concerns, offer 5-minute tech check call day before
- **Day of visit:** If patient doesn't join within 10 minutes, call (don't just mark as no-show)

**Success Metric:** Reduce technology-related virtual visit failures by 60% by end of Q4

---

### 🎯 Priority 3: Extend Appointment Hours for Working Patients

**Why This Matters:**
- 21% of patients reported work-hour conflicts
- Hourly workers lost wages to attend appointments (some reported losing medication money)
- Parents cited lack of weekend slots when partner could provide childcare

**Actions:**

| Action | Owner | Timeline |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Pilot evening clinic (5pm-7pm) twice weekly starting Week 3 | Operations Manager | Week 3-8 |
| Add Saturday morning slots (8am-12pm) once monthly | Scheduling Lead | Week 4 |
| Reserve 20% of slots for same-week availability | Clinical Director | Week 2 |
| Track no-show rates by time slot to optimize scheduling | Data/Analytics | Ongoing |

**What Frontline Staff Needs to Know:**
- **At scheduling:** Proactively offer evening slots to patients who mention work conflicts
- **Waitlist:** Maintain waitlist for evening/Saturday slots—these fill quickly
- **Documentation:** Note work schedule conflicts in patient record for future scheduling

**Success Metric:** Reduce work-related missed appointments by 40% by end of Q4

---

## What's NOT Changing (For Now)

Based on feasibility assessment, the following are **not** Q4 priorities but remain on our radar:

- **Transportation support:** We acknowledge this barrier (21% of patients) but will monitor before committing resources. Transportation voucher programs require budget approval and partner negotiations.
- **Full weekend clinic:** Saturday pilot will inform whether expanded weekend hours are sustainable.
- **Portal redesign:** Password/check-in issues are being addressed through simplified process, not full rebuild.

---

## How We'll Track Progress

**Weekly:**
- Barrier-related no-show counts (categorized by type)
- Interpreter availability issues
- Virtual visit technical failures

**Monthly:**
- Adherence rates by visit type (virtual vs in-person)
- Patient satisfaction scores related to access
- Staff feedback on new workflows

**End of Q4:**
- Full outcomes review with clinical metrics (A1C, BP control)
- Cost-benefit analysis of interventions
- Decision on which pilots to make permanent
